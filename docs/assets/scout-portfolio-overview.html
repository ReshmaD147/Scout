🤖Agentic Assistance Lane
  • ✓Intent splitter handles clear, compound, ambiguous, and out-of-scope requests.
  • ✓Supervisor is used only when deterministic routing is not enough.
  • ✓Recommendation, inventory, order, external offer, and policy specialists have code-selected tool allowlists.
  • ✓Only customer-safe progress events and verified responses are emitted.
🛒Deterministic Commerce Lane
  • ✓Product search/details and affiliate redirects are normal FastAPI routes.
  • ✓/cart/add validates product, stock, price, and promotion server-side.
  • ✓Cart update/remove/clear stays in React Context + localStorage.
  • ✓/checkout creates orders and Stripe PaymentIntents outside the agent path.
🛡️Trust Layer
  • ✓Tool calls create sanitized evidence records.
  • ✓Customer-visible facts are proposed, verified, and approved before rendering.
  • ✓Rejected facts are not edited out of model prose — replies are rebuilt from approved claims.
  • ✓One bounded correction attempt can run, then it re-enters the full verification pipeline.
🗄️Shared Services + Data
  • ✓Scout services and repositories are shared by both AI and deterministic paths.
  • ✓SQLite stores product, stock, store, order, promotion, affiliate, and external-offer data.
  • ✓Chroma + Ollama embeddings support policy and product retrieval.
